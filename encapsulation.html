Technology platforms

Choose the right carrier

Polycaprolactone (PCL) nanocapsules

Biodegradable, biocompatible polymer for sustained release. 200–400 nm, 89.6–99.5% encapsulation yield, +38% active concentration inside skin vs free active.

Special Chemicals

Alginate capsules

Natural algae polysaccharide formed by ionic gelation. Protects fragile actives from enzymes — over 50% activity retained at 72 h — and is safe even for lip products.

Special Chemicals

Lipid–alginate hybrid

Alginate core with a lecithin outer layer. Stays stable 28 days in a 30% hydroalcoholic base at 40 °C — ideal for serums and lotions.

Special Chemicals

Custom liposomes

Lipid composition, vesicle size, charge and release profile designed per project, with ceramides, sterols, sphingosine or exosome-like systems.

Special Chemicals

Microsponge® & Microsponge® N

10–20 µm porous microparticles (natural cellulose option, COSMOS certified) for sustained release and lower irritation of retinoids, acids, vitamin C, peptides and oils.

AMCOL Health & Beauty Solutions

Poly-Pore® & PolyTrap®

High-loading polymer systems that entrap liquids or solids and release on application while absorbing excess oil.

AMCOL Health & Beauty Solutions

Ziamozome™ targeted liposomes

Flexible liposomes targeted to melanocytes; up to 10% loading, >70% encapsulation, size <300 nm. Clinically tested on Asian skin.

Nabsolute / Dr.PONG

HyaSphere® nano-carriers

Hyaluronic-acid nano-encapsulation that improves solubility, stability, penetration and controlled release.
