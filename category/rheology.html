Frequently asked questions

What is magnesium aluminium silicate used for in cosmetics?

As a natural thickener, suspending agent and emulsion stabiliser in creams, lotions, sunscreens and masks.

Is Magnabrite an alternative to Veegum?

Both are magnesium aluminium silicate; Magnabrite® is the HBS (AMCOL) brand, with grades matched by viscosity and acid or electrolyte tolerance.

Are clay thickeners natural?

Yes. Magnabrite® and Polargel® are mined minerals, purified to USP/NF grade, with no animal or plant origin.
