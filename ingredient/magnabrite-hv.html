About Magnabrite® HV

Magnabrite® HV is the thickest grade of HBS's magnesium aluminium silicate. It meets USP/NF Type IC and reaches 800–2200 cps at 5% solids, roughly three to four times the viscosity of Magnabrite® S at the same level. That lets formulators build texture with less clay, keeping the skin feel light.

It works as a stabiliser, emulsifier and suspending agent and is supplied as flakes for easy dispersion.

Typical uses

  • Creams and lotions that need more body
  • Roll-on antiperspirants and deodorants
  • Acne treatments and suspensions
  • Low-solids gels paired with xanthan gum

Key benefits

  • Higher viscosity grade

Frequently asked questions

Which Magnabrite grade has the highest viscosity?

Magnabrite® HV, at 800–2200 cps for a 5% dispersion.

How is Magnabrite® HV different from Magnabrite® S?

Both share the same Al/Mg ratio (0.5–1.2), but HV is processed for much higher viscosity, so it is used where more body is wanted at a low dose.
