FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which principals does Kalptaru Naturals represent in India?

We represent Special Chemicals (SCH, Spain), AMCOL Health & Beauty Solutions (HBS, USA), Bielus Ingredientes (Brazil), A&PEP and Daebong LS (South Korea), Nabsolute (Thailand), Namib Desert Oils (Namibia), Transactiva (Italy), Gaia Tech (Switzerland), Nutrition21 / Everwell Health (USA) and Suzhou Greenway Biotech (China).

Can I get samples, TDS, MSDS and CoA?

Yes. Add ingredients to your enquiry list on any product page and send it on WhatsApp, or email ChandraBDM@kalptarunaturals.in. We share the manufacturer's TDS, MSDS and CoA with samples.

Do you offer custom encapsulation?

Yes. With Special Chemicals we design PCL nanocapsules, alginate, lipid–alginate hybrids or custom liposomes around your active, skin target and claim, and HBS Microsponge® systems cover ready-made needs.

Which ingredients do you offer for skin brightening?

Encapsulated tranexamic acid (SCH ShellCare® TRAX range), TranEX-1, nonapeptide-1, vitamin C derivatives, glutathione, arbutin, kojic dipalmitate and Ziamozome™ melanocyte-targeted liposomes.

Do you supply hair-growth actives?

Yes — copper peptides (Actigrowth-10, GHK-Cu), biotinoyl tripeptide-1, SCH ShellCare® Hair Grow, Scalp and Antigrey, Vital Growth-1, Lavieen-F and Indian botanicals such as bhringraj and saw palmetto.

Where is Kalptaru Naturals located?

139, Preet Colony, Jandali, Ambala City, Haryana 134003, India. We supply cosmetic manufacturers and brands across India.
